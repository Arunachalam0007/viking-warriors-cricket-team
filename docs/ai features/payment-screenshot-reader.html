AI feature

Payment screenshot reader

Admins upload or paste a GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / bank screenshot; the AI reads amount, date, payer and UPI reference, guesses the player, and fills the Record-payment form.

Purpose and access

read Admins: Admin → Payments → Scan screenshot. Nothing is saved until the admin presses Record payment as usual. The image is not stored.

Flow

flowchart LR
  U[Choose / drop / paste screenshot] --> Z[Browser shrinks it: max 1600 px JPEG]
  Z --> A["scanPaymentScreenshot (server action, admin only)"]
  A --> V["generateText + image + Output.object (vision chain: Gemini models first)"]
  V --> X[amount, date, app, payer, payee, reference, status, matched player]
  X --> C[Checks: failed / pending, paise rounding, future date, duplicate reference, guest, already paid up]
  C --> D[Dialog: details + warnings]
  D -- Use these details --> F[Record payment form filled]
  F -- admin presses Record payment --> P[(payments + allocations)]

What the checks warn about

  • Status FAILED or PENDING on the screenshot.
  • Amount with paise (rounded to whole rupees).
  • No clear player match, or only a medium / low confidence one.
  • The player is a guest (guests are not charged).
  • The same UPI reference is already recorded (possible duplicate).
  • The player owes nothing (the money would become advance credit).
