Admin page

Revenue overview

Admin home: everything the team has charged, collected and spent, who holds the money, top dues and sync health.

Purpose and access

admin only

Admins only. proxy.ts redirects anonymous visitors to /login; the page calls requireAdmin() before loading data, and every action re-checks through adminAction().

URL and query parameters

URL / parameterMeaning
/adminNo parameters.

What is shown

  • Tiles — Fund balance (collected + income − expenses), Outstanding dues (sum of positive player balances), Collected (all payments), Fees charged (non-waived charges), Player credit (advance payments), Other income, Expenses, Cash in hand (all holders).
  • Month by month chart — charged, collected, expenses, other income per month.
  • Cash in hand — per cash holder (Rahul by default): received from players, income, spent, in hand.
  • Top dues — 10 players who owe the most, linking to Payments.
  • Sync status — last CricHeroes and Google Sheet run with a Sync CricHeroes now button.
  • Admin pages quick links and Download full admin Excel (/api/export/finance?scope=admin).
