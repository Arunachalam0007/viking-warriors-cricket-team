Public page

Reports

Season trends: results, runs and wickets by month, match fees charged vs collected, and the season top-performers table.

Purpose and access

public

Open to everyone; no login. Pages are rendered on the server on every request from the app database (never from CricHeroes directly).

URL and query parameters

URL / parameterMeaning
/reportsNo parameters; season = sync_start_date to today.

What is shown

  • Results by month — stacked won / lost bars (getMonthlyTeamTrends; abandoned not shown).
  • Runs & wickets by month — team totals from our players.
  • Match fees by month — charged vs collected (from getRevenueSummary().monthly; expenses, income and cash holders are not shown publicly).
  • Season top performers — matches, runs, average, strike rate, wickets, economy, catches, MVP.
