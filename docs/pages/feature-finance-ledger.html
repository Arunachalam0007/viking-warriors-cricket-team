Finance ledger

How charges, payments, allocations, credit and statuses work, and the functions that change them.

Purpose and access

feature

What is shown

Concepts

  • Charge (charges) — something a player owes: match_fee (one per match + player), collection (one per collection + player) or adjustment.
  • Payment (payments) — money received; source = quick (from "Mark paid") or manual (lump sum).
  • Allocation (payment_allocations) — part of a payment applied to a charge.
  • Balance = Σ non-waived charges − Σ payments. Negative = credit.
  • Status (chargeStatus) — waived, paid (allocated ≥ amount), partial, unpaid.

Rules and edge cases

  • Money is whole rupees (integers) everywhere.
  • Every ledger change made by an admin runs in one transaction with an audit row.
  • Correlated subqueries use explicit table-qualified names (drizzle drops prefixes in single-table selects).
